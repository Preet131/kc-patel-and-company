What is the standard timeline for Trademark Registration in India?

Once a Trademark application (TM-A) is filed, you receive an official application number immediately, allowing the use of the ™ symbol. The Trade Marks Registry examines the mark within 1 to 3 months. If no objections or oppositions are raised during the mandatory 4-month journal publication period, the registration certificate (®) is typically issued within 6 to 12 months.

Why is an exhaustive Patent Search essential prior to drafting?

A comprehensive prior art search across Indian and international patent databases determines whether an invention satisfies the statutory criteria of novelty, inventive step (non-obviousness), and industrial applicability under the Patents Act, 1970. This prevents unnecessary expenditure on unpatentable ideas and helps structure technical claims effectively.

What is the legal distinction between Trademark, Patent, and Copyright?

Trademark protects brand identities, names, logos, and slogans. Patent protects technological innovations, functional devices, chemical formulas, and industrial processes. Copyright protects original artistic, literary, musical, and software code expressions from unauthorized reproduction.

What documents are required to apply for an Import Export Code (IEC)?

Key requirements include the Business Entity's PAN Card, Bank Account cancelled cheque, proof of business premises (rent agreement or utility bill), and Director/Proprietor ID proof. Our compliance desk manages complete digital submission to DGFT.
