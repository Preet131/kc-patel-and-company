What is the standard timeline for Trademark Registration in India?

Once a Trademark application (TM-A) is filed, an official application number is issued. The Trade Marks Registry examines the mark within 1 to 3 months. If no objections or oppositions are raised during the mandatory 4-month journal publication period, the registration certificate (®) is typically issued within 6 to 12 months.

Why is a Patent Search conducted prior to drafting?

A prior art search across patent databases evaluates whether an invention meets statutory criteria of novelty, inventive step (non-obviousness), and industrial applicability under the Patents Act, 1970, assisting in proper claim structuring.

What is the distinction between Trademark, Patent, and Copyright?

Trademark applies to brand identifiers, names, logos, and slogans. Patent applies to functional devices, technical inventions, and industrial processes. Copyright applies to original literary, artistic, musical, and software code expressions.

What documents are required for Import Export Code (IEC) registration?

Standard documentation includes the Business Entity's PAN Card, Bank Account cancelled cheque, proof of business premises, and Director/Proprietor ID proof for submission to the DGFT portal.
